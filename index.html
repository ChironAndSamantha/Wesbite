How to save photos on your device

iPhone / iPad: Open a photo and tap Save / Share, then choose Save Image if available. You can also open the original, touch and hold the image, and choose Save to Photos. Downloads may appear in Files → Downloads.

Android: Tap Download original, or use Save / Share to send it to a supported app. Downloads are usually in Files → Downloads.

PC / Mac: Tap Download original. Your browser will save the photo to your downloads folder or ask where to put it.
