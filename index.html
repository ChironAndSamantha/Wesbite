Our Story

Some love stories happen exactly as planned. Ours took a little longer to find its way.

Nine years ago, we met and shared a connection that neither of us forgot. Although we dated briefly, life had other plans for us at the time, and we went our separate ways. Looking back now, it seems we both had growing to do, lessons to learn, and journeys to take before we were truly ready for the life we would eventually build together.


Years later, in 2021, fate brought us back to one another through a simple online reconnection. What started as a conversation quickly felt like coming home. Despite the years apart, the connection was still there, stronger than ever, and it didn't take long for us to realise that this was something too special to let slip away again.


In 2022, we made our relationship official, and just six months later, we took the leap and moved in together. It felt effortless, as though life was simply falling into place exactly as it was meant to. Soon after, our little family grew with the addition of three beautiful cats, who filled our home with even more love, laughter, chaos, and countless cuddles.


Since then, we've shared adventures both big and small, celebrated milestones, supported each other through challenges, and built a home that is full of happiness, comfort, and unconditional love. The everyday moments, quiet mornings, shared dreams, late-night laughs, and everything in between have become our favourite part of this journey.


What began as a brief chapter nine years ago became the love story of a lifetime. Sometimes the universe has a funny way of bringing two people back together when the timing is finally right.


Today, we couldn't be more excited to celebrate this next chapter with the people we love most. Thank you for being part of our story, and for joining us as we celebrate our engagement and the future that lies ahead.
